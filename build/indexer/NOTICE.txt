Prism includes Everything 1.4.1.1032 (x64) by David Carpenter / voidtools
and the Everything command-line interface ES 1.1.0.38 (x64).

Everything: https://www.voidtools.com/
ES source: https://github.com/voidtools/es

Redistribution notices are in Everything-LICENSE.txt and ES-LICENSE.txt.
The Everything notice also covers its bundled Perl-Compatible Regular Expressions.

Prism uses its own named index and Windows service. It does not change an existing
Everything installation, settings, index, service, shortcuts, or startup entries.
The optional privileged service executable lives in Program Files, separate from
the per-user Prism application. Declining Windows administrator approval keeps
Prism usable with its own unprivileged folder index and filesystem fallback.
