the second file One process. Open another file from anywhere and the window already up takes it, with no second start-up to sit through.
a frame at a time Comma and period walk single frames. Arrows jump five seconds. Space stops it dead.
or faster Speed sits in the transport, where you are already looking, not three pages into a settings tree.
rename it in place F2, type, enter. The extension is left alone unless you go looking for it.
delete, reversibly It asks once and says where the file goes. Everything Prism removes lands in the Recycle Bin, overwrites included.
and the visualizer Glow, cycle, drift. Every switch changes what you are looking at while the track keeps playing.
